# APPS TRAIN 0312

Return the length of the shortest, non-empty, contiguous subarray of A with sum at least K.
If there is no non-empty subarray with sum at least K, return -1.
 



Example 1:
Input: A = [1], K = 1
Output: 1


Example 2:
Input: A = [1,2], K = 4
Output: -1


Example 3:
Input: A = [2,-1,2], K = 3
Output: 3

 
Note:

1 <= A.length <= 50000
-10 ^ 5 <= A[i] <= 10 ^ 5
1 <= K <= 10 ^ 9
